About

Who I am.

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer turned AI Researcher — building generative models that work under real-world constraints.

I graduated from RUET, Bangladesh and currently work as an independent research collaborator with a professor at the University of Queensland, Australia.

My work spans diffusion models, frequency-domain learning, knowledge distillation, and federated learning, with a focus on developing efficient AI methods for real-world computational constraints.

I've collaborated individually with researchers across Australia, Japan, and Bangladesh, and I'm currently preparing to pursue PhD research in North America.
